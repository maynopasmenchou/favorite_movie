THE STORY BEHIND THE MOVIE

ABOUT COCO

A Journey Beyond Life

Coco is a 2017 animated film produced by Pixar Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures.

The story follows Miguel Rivera, a young boy who dreams of becoming a musician despite his family's ban on music. Through an unexpected adventure in the Land of the Dead, Miguel discovers the truth about his family and the importance of remembering the people we love.

The movie beautifully shows Mexican culture, family traditions, music, and the meaning of remembering our loved ones.

MY PERSONAL THOUGHTS

How Coco Affected My Life

Coco is one of my favorite movie because it teaches me the importance of family, love, and following my dreams. While watching the movie, I realized that no matter how difficult life becomes, I should never forget the people who love and support me.

The story of Miguel inspired me to follow my dreams even when there are challenges along the way. It also taught me to appreciate my family, spend more time with them, and make memories while we are together.

For me, Coco is more than just an animated movie. It reminds me that family will always be important and that the memories we share will stay in our hearts.
