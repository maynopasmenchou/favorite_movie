THE STORY BEHIND THE MOVIE

ABOUT COCO

A Journey Beyond Life

Coco is a 2017 animated film produced by Pixar Animation Studios.

The story follows Miguel Rivera, a young boy who dreams of becoming a musician.

The movie beautifully shows Mexican culture, family traditions, music, and memories.
